## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels in FastAPI Hypermedia Templates\n**Learning:** The 'future.html' and 'cj_template.html' templates in this project contain several interactive elements (buttons, inputs) that lack appropriate ARIA labels or semantic names, making them difficult to use with screen readers.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label` attributes to icon-only buttons (like the close button in 'future.html') and ensure all form inputs have proper accessible names.
